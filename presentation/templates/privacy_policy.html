Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

1. Data Controller

The Organ Donation System is operated as an academic project at Technological University Dublin. For any data protection queries, please contact the project administrator through the university.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Data Category Examples Protection
PII Name, Address AES-256 encryption at rest (pgcrypto)
Medical Health information, organ preferences AES-256 encryption at rest (pgcrypto)
Demographic Age Stored in database
Authentication Username, Password Password hashed with PBKDF2 (irreversible)

3. Why We Collect Your Data

We process your personal data based on your explicit consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)) for the following purposes:

  • To allow you to register and complete organ donation questionnaires
  • To share your anonymised questionnaire responses with the requesting organisation
  • To maintain a tamper-evident audit trail for accountability (GDPR Article 5(2))
  • To enable you to exercise your data protection rights

4. Who Can See Your Data

  • You can view all your data at any time via "Right to Access"
  • Client organisations can only see your anonymised questionnaire answers (displayed as "Respondent 1", "Respondent 2", etc.) and only while your consent is active
  • No one outside this system receives your personal data

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

Your personal data is retained for as long as you maintain an active account. You may delete your data or your entire account at any time. Upon deletion:

  • Account deletion: All personal data, questionnaire answers, and submissions are permanently removed
  • Submission deletion: Answer data is permanently erased; a minimal audit record is retained for accountability
  • Consent withdrawal: Your data is hidden from the organisation but preserved in case you wish to reinstate consent

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the following rights, all of which can be exercised directly within this system:

Right to Access

View all personal data stored about you (Article 15)

Right to Rectification

Edit and correct your questionnaire answers (Article 16)

Right to Erasure

Delete your account and all associated data (Article 17)

Right to Portability

Download all your data as a CSV file (Article 20)

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent per organisation at any time (Article 7(3))

Right to Restrict Processing

Withdraw consent to hide your data from organisations (Article 18)

7. How We Protect Your Data

  • Encryption at rest: All PII and medical data is encrypted using AES-256 via PostgreSQL pgcrypto
  • Password hashing: Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2 and cannot be recovered
  • Tamper-evident audit trail: All data access is logged with SHA-256 hash chaining for integrity verification
  • Access control: Users can only access their own data; organisations see anonymised responses only

8. Cookies

This system uses a single strictly necessary session cookie to keep you logged in. This cookie:

  • Is deleted when you log out or close your browser
  • Does not track you across other websites
  • Is required for the system to function and is exempt from consent under the ePrivacy Directive

9. Contact & Complaints

If you have concerns about how your data is handled, you may:

  • Contact the project administrator through Technological University Dublin
  • Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission (DPC) of Ireland at www.dataprotection.ie
