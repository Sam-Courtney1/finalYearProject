Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation

1. What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Data Category Examples Protection
PII Name, Address AES-256 encryption at rest
Medical Health information, organ preferences AES-256 encryption at rest
Demographic Age Stored in database
Authentication Username, Password Password hashed with scrypt default

2. Why We Collect Your Data

We process your personal data based on your explicit consent for the following purposes:

  • To allow you to register and complete organ donation questionnaires
  • To share your anonymised questionnaire responses with the requesting organisation
  • To maintain an audit trail for accountability
  • To enable you to exercise your data protection rights

3. Who Can See Your Data

  • You can view all your data at any time via "Right to Access"
  • Client organisations can only see your anonymised questionnaire answers and only while your consent is active
  • No one outside this system receives your personal data

4. How Long We Keep Your Data

Your personal data is retained for as long as you maintain an active account. You may delete your data or your entire account at any time. Upon deletion:

  • Account deletion: All personal data, questionnaire answers, and submissions are permanently removed
  • Submission deletion: Answer data is permanently erased; a minimal audit record is retained for accountability
  • Consent withdrawal: Your data is hidden from the organisation but preserved in case you wish to reinstate consent

Data Retention Policy

In accordance with GDPR, we apply the following retention rules:

  • Inactive accounts: Accounts with no login activity for 12 months are subject to data cleanup
  • Expired submissions: Questionnaire submissions older than the retention period may be removed during reviews
  • Audit logs: Retained separately for regulatory compliance and are not subject to user-data retention limits

5. Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach:

  • Affected individuals will be notified within 72 hours of a breach occuring
  • The notification will include: the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of individuals affected, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address the breach

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the following rights, all of which can be exercised directly within this system:

Right to Access

View all personal data stored about you

Right to Rectification

Edit and correct your questionnaire answers

Right to Erasure

Delete your account and all associated data

Right to Portability

Download all your data as a CSV file

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent per organisation at any time

Right to Restrict Processing

Withdraw consent to hide your data from organisations

7. How We Protect Your Data

  • Encryption at rest: All PII and medical data is encrypted using AES-256 via PostgreSQL pgcrypto
  • Password hashing: Passwords are hashed and cannot be recovered
  • Tamper-evident audit trail: All data access is logged with SHA-256 hash chaining for integrity verification
  • Access control: Users can only access their own data, organisations see anonymised responses only
